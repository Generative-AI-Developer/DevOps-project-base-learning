# Handling Complaints and Difficult Conversations

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to handle an upset customer or colleague calmly and solve the problem.

## 2. Real-world scenario
A customer is angry because their order is late. You must apologize, understand their feeling, and offer a clear solution — without arguing.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Apologizing well.
- Showing understanding.
- Offering solutions and assuring.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lesson 2 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/03-complaints.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **complaint conversation** (8–10 lines): a person complains, and you handle it well.
2. Include: an apology, a line that shows you understand, a **specific solution**, and an assurance.
3. Also write a short **apology email/reply** (3–5 sentences) for the same situation.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Apology + understanding.
- [ ] A specific solution ("Here's what I can do…").
- [ ] An assurance ("I assure you…").
- [ ] No arguing or blaming.
- [ ] Use at least **7 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can calm an upset person and turn a complaint into a solution.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation apologizes and shows understanding of the person's feeling.
2. It offers a **specific** solution (not vague).
3. It includes an assurance and never argues or blames.
4. Your apology email covers sorry + reason + solution.
5. You use at least **7 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the tone stays calm and polite.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Apology + understanding.
- [ ] Specific solution + assurance.
- [ ] Apology email written.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-03/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "It's not my fault."
- Arguing back.
- No solution offered.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Apologize: "I'm truly sorry for the delay and the inconvenience."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Understand: "I understand how you feel — that's frustrating."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Solution: "Here's what I can do: I'll send it today, free of charge."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write one line for a **difficult colleague** situation (not a customer): politely raise a problem without blaming, e.g., "Can we talk about the report? I think we can improve how we share updates."

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your conversation + apology email in `submissions/english/advanced/project-03/`. Then say: **"I submit English Advanced Project 3."**
