# English — Advanced Level

Now you build **confident, professional English** for higher situations: presentations, negotiation, complaints, reports, and interviews.

> **In simple words:** you learn to speak and write clearly and strongly in important work moments.

The focus is still **vocabulary** and **practical use**, with a little grammar. Every lesson has a vocabulary table with **English and Urdu (اردو)** meanings, strong professional phrases, and a real situation.

## The 8 lessons
1. Presentations — explaining ideas clearly
2. Persuasion and negotiation phrases
3. Handling complaints and difficult conversations politely
4. Writing reports and documentation
5. Job interviews — talking about your skills and experience
6. Networking and professional etiquette
7. Advanced email and written communication (tone, clarity, formality)
8. Job interview + follow-up email (level boss)

## Exit criteria
Finish all 8 lessons to complete the **whole English track**. You will then handle daily life and office communication with confidence.

**Remember:** Confidence comes from practice. Say your lines out loud and write real messages. You are almost fluent for the workplace. 🌟
