# Job Interviews — Skills and Experience

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to answer common interview questions with confidence and real examples.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You have a job interview. You must answer "Tell me about yourself", "What is your strength?", "What is your weakness?", and "Why should we hire you?"

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Talking about experience (present perfect).
- Strengths with examples.
- Honest weakness answers.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lesson 4 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/05-job-interviews.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write **your answers** to these 4 questions: "Tell me about yourself", "What is your greatest strength?", "What is a weakness?", "Why should we hire you?"
2. Each answer should be 2–4 sentences.
3. Include at least one **example** (achievement) and use present perfect for experience.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Answers to all 4 questions.
- [ ] One strength with an example.
- [ ] An honest weakness + how you improve it.
- [ ] Present perfect used for experience.
- [ ] Use at least **8 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
Confident, honest, well-structured interview answers you could really use.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. You answered all 4 questions (each 2–4 sentences).
2. Your strength answer includes a real example.
3. Your weakness answer is honest and shows improvement (not fake).
4. You use present perfect correctly for experience ("I have worked…").
5. You use at least **8 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the tone is confident but not boastful.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] 4 answers.
- [ ] Strength example + honest weakness.
- [ ] Present perfect used.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-05/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Too boastful.
- Fake weakness ("I work too hard").
- "since 3 years" → "for 3 years" / "have worked".

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Yourself: "I'm a ___ with ___ years of experience. I have ___."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Strength: "My strength is ___. For example, ___."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Weakness: "I used to ___. Now I have learned to ___."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write your answer to "Where do you see yourself in 3 years?" using a clear goal: "In three years, I hope to ___."
