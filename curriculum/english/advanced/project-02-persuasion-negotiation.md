# Persuasion and Negotiation

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to persuade with benefits and negotiate a fair, win-win deal.

## 2. Real-world scenario
A client wants a very short deadline. You must negotiate a fair plan that keeps quality — without saying a hard "no".

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Persuading with benefits.
- Negotiating and compromising.
- Conditional offers ("If…, we can…").

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lesson 1 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/02-persuasion-negotiation.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **negotiation conversation** (8–10 lines) about a deadline, price, or plan.
2. Include: understanding their concern, a **benefit**, a **compromise** ("meet halfway"), and an **"If…, we can…"** offer.
3. End with a win-win agreement.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Show you understand their concern.
- [ ] State a clear benefit.
- [ ] Offer a compromise.
- [ ] Use an "If…, we can…" offer.
- [ ] Use at least **7 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
A polite, effective negotiation that reaches a win-win.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation starts by understanding the other side's concern.
2. It states a clear benefit and offers a compromise ("meet halfway").
3. It includes one "If…, we can…" conditional offer.
4. It ends with a win-win agreement.
5. You use at least **7 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the tone is polite.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Concern + benefit + compromise.
- [ ] "If…, we can…" used.
- [ ] Win-win ending.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-02/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Being pushy ("You must…").
- No benefit given.
- Not listening to their concern.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Start: "I understand your concern about time. However, …"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Compromise: "How about we meet halfway?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Offer: "If you give me two more days, we can improve the quality."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write one line that firmly but politely holds your position: "I'm afraid I can't go lower than that, but I can add [extra] for the same price."
