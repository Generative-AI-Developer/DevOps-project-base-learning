# Networking and Professional Etiquette

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to meet new professional people politely and follow up to build relationships.

## 2. Real-world scenario
At a work event (or online), you meet a useful new contact. You greet them well, exchange details, and follow up.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Warm, professional greetings.
- Exchanging contacts / connecting.
- Writing a follow-up message.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lesson 5 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/06-networking-etiquette.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **networking conversation** (6–8 lines) where you meet a new professional contact.
2. Write a short **follow-up message** (3–4 sentences) you would send the next day.
3. Include an offer of value (help/refer), not just a request.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A polite networking conversation.
- [ ] Exchange contacts / ask to connect.
- [ ] A follow-up message.
- [ ] An offer of value.
- [ ] Use at least **7 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can make a strong first impression and follow up professionally.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation greets warmly and professionally ("It's a pleasure to meet you").
2. It includes exchanging details or asking to connect.
3. Your follow-up message is polite and mentions staying in touch.
4. You include an offer of value (help/refer).
5. You use at least **7 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the tone is warm and professional.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Warm greeting.
- [ ] Connect/exchange.
- [ ] Follow-up message.
- [ ] Offer of value.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-06/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Too casual with new contacts.
- No follow-up.
- Only asking, never giving.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Greet: "Hello, I'm ___. It's a pleasure to meet you."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Connect: "May I connect with you on LinkedIn? Let's stay in touch."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Follow-up: "Hi ___, great to meet you today. I'd be happy to help if you need ___. Best, ___."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write a short, polite LinkedIn connection note (2 sentences) to someone you met once.
