# Presentations — Explaining Ideas Clearly

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks when English Advanced opens)

## 1. Objective
Learn to structure and deliver a short, clear presentation.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You must present a small idea or plan to your team in 3 minutes. It should be clear, structured, and confident.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Opening, body, and conclusion.
- Signpost words (First / Next / Finally).
- Points with examples.

## 4. Prerequisites
- English Intermediate completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/01-presentations.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Choose a topic (a plan, an idea, or something you know well).
2. Write a **mini-presentation script** (12–16 lines): an opening, **three points** (each with an example), and a conclusion.
3. Use signpost words (First / Next / Finally) and invite questions at the end.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Clear opening (what you'll cover).
- [ ] Three points, each with an example.
- [ ] Signpost words used.
- [ ] A conclusion + "Any questions?"
- [ ] Use at least **8 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
A clear, structured mini-presentation you could deliver with confidence.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your script has an opening that says what you'll cover.
2. It has three points, each with an example.
3. It uses signpost words (First / Next / Finally).
4. It ends with a conclusion and invites questions.
5. You use at least **8 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the English is clear.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Opening + 3 points + examples.
- [ ] Signposting.
- [ ] Conclusion + questions.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-01/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- No structure.
- Points with no examples.
- No conclusion.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Open: "Today I'll talk about ___. I'll cover three points: first…, next…, finally…"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Each point: "The first point is ___. For example, ___."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Close: "In conclusion, ___. Are there any questions?"</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Practice saying your presentation out loud 3 times, then write one sentence about what felt hard and how you improved it.

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your presentation script in `submissions/english/advanced/project-01/`. Then say: **"I submit English Advanced Project 1."**
