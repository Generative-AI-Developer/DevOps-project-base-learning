# Job Interview + Follow-Up Email (Level Boss 🏆🏆)

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

This is the **final English lesson**. Finish it to complete the **whole English track**. It brings together interviews, professional writing, tone, and etiquette.

## 1. Objective
Do a full written mock interview and write a professional follow-up email.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You interview for a job you want. You answer the key questions well, then send a polite thank-you email that repeats your interest.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Complete interview answers with examples.
- A professional follow-up email.
- The full application flow.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lessons 1–7 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/08-interview-followup.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
**Part A — Interview (written):** Answer these 5 questions (2–4 sentences each):
1. Tell me about yourself.
2. What is your greatest strength? (with an example)
3. What is a weakness? (honest + how you improve it)
4. Why do you want to work here?
5. Do you have any questions for us? (write 2 good questions to ask)

**Part B — Follow-up email:** Write a professional thank-you email (subject + greeting + 4–6 sentence body + closing) that thanks the interviewer and repeats your interest.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] All 5 interview answers.
- [ ] One strength with an example; one honest weakness.
- [ ] Two smart questions to ask them.
- [ ] A complete follow-up email.
- [ ] Use at least **10 words** from this level's lessons.

## 7. Expected outcome
A complete, confident interview and a polished follow-up email — job-ready English.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. You answered all 5 interview questions, each 2–4 sentences.
2. Your strength answer has a real example; your weakness answer is honest and shows improvement.
3. You wrote 2 good questions to ask the interviewer.
4. Your follow-up email has a subject, greeting, a body that thanks them and repeats interest, and a closing.
5. You use at least **10 vocabulary words** from this level, present perfect for experience, and a polite, confident tone.
6. The English is clear and proofread (no careless typos).

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] 5 interview answers.

## 10. Common mistakes
- No follow-up email.
- Fake weakness.
- No questions to ask ("No, I have no questions" looks unprepared).
- Typos — proofread!
