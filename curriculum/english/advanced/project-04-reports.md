# Writing Reports and Documentation

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to write a clear, structured short report with headings and recommendations.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your manager asks for a short report on something you did (a month's work, a small project, a result). It must be clear, concise, and organized.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Report structure and headings.
- Formal, concise tone.
- Findings, conclusion, and recommendations.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Advanced Lesson 3 completed.
- Read: `study/english/advanced/04-reports.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Choose a real or example topic.
2. Write a **short report** with clear headings: **Introduction** (purpose), **Findings** (2–3 points), **Conclusion**, and **Recommendations** (1–2 actions).
3. Use a formal, concise tone.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Clear section headings.
- [ ] A purpose line in the introduction.
- [ ] 2–3 findings.
- [ ] A conclusion + 1–2 recommendations.
- [ ] Use at least **8 words** from today's list.
- [ ] Formal tone (avoid "I think"; use "The data shows…").

## 7. Expected outcome
A short, clear, professional report a manager could read quickly and act on.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your report has clear headings (Introduction, Findings, Conclusion, Recommendations).
2. The introduction states the purpose; there are 2–3 findings.
3. There is a conclusion and at least one recommendation.
4. The tone is formal and concise (mostly avoids "I think").
5. You use at least **8 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the English is clear.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Headings + purpose.
- [ ] Findings + conclusion + recommendation.
- [ ] Formal tone.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/advanced/project-04/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- One long block, no headings.
- Too long / not concise.
- "I think…" everywhere.
