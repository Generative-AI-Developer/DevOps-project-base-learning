# Run a Short Meeting (Level Boss 🏆)

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

This is the **last lesson** of English Intermediate. Finish it to complete the level and unlock English Advanced. It brings together emails, meetings, updates, and requests.

## 1. Objective
Write and "run" a short meeting: open it, follow an agenda, assign tasks, and recap.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your small team is behind on a project. You lead a 5-minute meeting to solve it: share the status, assign tasks with deadlines, and recap.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Opening and leading a meeting.
- Assigning tasks (person + deadline).
- Recapping clearly.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lessons 1–7 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/08-run-a-meeting.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **meeting script** (12–16 lines) where YOU are the chair.
2. It must: **open** the meeting, state a short **agenda**, discuss a problem, **assign at least 2 tasks** (each with a person and a deadline), and end with a **recap** and a **wrap-up**.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Clear opening + agenda.
- [ ] Discussion of a problem.
- [ ] 2+ tasks assigned (person + deadline each).
- [ ] A recap (who does what, by when).
- [ ] Use at least **8 words** from today's list.
- [ ] Use "Let's + verb" at least twice.

## 7. Expected outcome
A short, clear meeting script that solves a problem and ends with everyone knowing their tasks.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your script opens the meeting and states an agenda.
2. It assigns at least **2 tasks**, each with a **person** and a **deadline**.
3. It ends with a clear **recap** of the action items.
4. You use "Let's + verb" at least twice.
5. You use at least **8 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the English is clear and polite.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Open + agenda.
- [ ] 2+ tasks with person + deadline.
- [ ] Recap + wrap up.
- [ ] 8+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-08/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- No recap at the end.
- Tasks without a person or a deadline.
- "Let's to start" → "Let's start".

## 12. Final challenge
Turn the recap into a short **follow-up email** to the team after the meeting (subject + action items + deadlines).

Save your meeting script (and optional follow-up email) in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-08/`. Then say: **"I submit English Intermediate Project 8."** When it passes, English Advanced unlocks! 🎉
