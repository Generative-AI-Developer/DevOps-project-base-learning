# Making and Handling Requests Politely

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to ask for things politely, offer help, and say "no" kindly with an alternative.

## 2. Real-world scenario
A colleague asks you to finish something by a time you can't manage. You must refuse politely and offer another time — and also ask them for a favor.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Soft requests ("Would you mind…?").
- Offering help.
- Polite refusal + alternative.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 5 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/06-requests.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **conversation** (8–10 lines) where: you ask a favor politely, the colleague agrees, and then you politely **refuse** their request and offer an **alternative**.
2. Include at least **2 polite requests** and **1 polite refusal with an alternative**.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] 2 polite requests ("Would you mind…?" / "Could you possibly…?").
- [ ] 1 offer of help.
- [ ] 1 polite refusal + alternative.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.
- [ ] Correct "Would you mind + -ing".

## 7. Expected outcome
You can ask, offer, and refuse — all politely — keeping good relationships.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation has 2 polite requests and 1 offer of help.
2. It has a polite refusal that includes an alternative ("…but I can… instead").
3. You use "Would you mind + -ing" correctly at least once.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The tone stays polite and the English is clear.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Requests + offer + refusal-with-alternative.
- [ ] "Would you mind + -ing" correct.
- [ ] 6+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-06/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "Would you mind to help" → "Would you mind helping".
- Refusing with just "No."
- Forgetting the alternative.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Request: "Would you mind checking my report?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Refuse: "Unfortunately I can't by 2, but I can by 4. Would that work?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Offer: "I'd be happy to help with the slides."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write one line asking for **permission** politely: "May I leave 30 minutes early today? I'll finish my tasks first."
