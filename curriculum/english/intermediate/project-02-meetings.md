# Meetings — Joining and Speaking Up

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to speak up in a meeting: agree, disagree politely, and suggest ideas.

## 2. Real-world scenario
In a team meeting, you have a different idea about a deadline. You must share it politely and clearly.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Polite agreeing and disagreeing.
- Suggesting ideas.
- Asking for clarity.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 1 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/02-meetings.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a short **meeting conversation** (7–9 lines) about a real topic (a deadline, a plan, or a problem).
2. In it, include one line where you **agree**, one where you **disagree politely**, and one where you **suggest** an idea.
3. Add one line asking someone to **clarify**.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A 7–9 line meeting conversation.
- [ ] One agree + one polite disagree + one suggestion.
- [ ] One "clarify" question.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can join a meeting and share your views politely and clearly.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation includes a polite agree, a polite disagree ("I see your point, but…"), and a suggestion.
2. It includes a request to clarify.
3. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
4. The tone is polite (no rude "you're wrong").
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Agree + disagree + suggest.
- [ ] Clarify question.
- [ ] 6+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-02/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "I am agree" → "I agree".
- Rude disagreement.
- Interrupting without "May I add something?"

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Agree: "I agree that the client is important."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Disagree: "I see your point, but Friday is too soon."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Suggest: "I'd like to suggest Monday instead."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Add a one-line **summary** at the end, as if you are the chair: "So, we will confirm with the client, then decide the date."

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your meeting conversation in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-02/`. Then say: **"I submit English Intermediate Project 2."**
