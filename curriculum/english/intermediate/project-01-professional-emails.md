# Professional Emails

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks when English Intermediate opens)

## 1. Objective
Write a clear, polite, professional email that asks for something and gets a good reply.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You must email a colleague to send a report and confirm a deadline. It must be professional and easy to act on.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Professional email structure and tone.
- Polite request language (could/would/kindly).
- Writing a reply and a follow-up.

## 4. Prerequisites
- English Beginner completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/01-professional-emails.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a professional **email** (subject + greeting + body + closing) that makes a clear request (e.g., ask for a report by a deadline).
2. Write a short **reply** to it.
3. Write a one-line **follow-up** (a polite reminder).

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A professional email with a clear request.
- [ ] A short reply.
- [ ] A polite follow-up line.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.
- [ ] Polite tone (could/would/kindly).

## 7. Expected outcome
A clear, polite email that a colleague could act on, plus a reply and a reminder.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your email has subject, greeting, body, and closing, and makes a clear request with a deadline.
2. The tone is polite (uses could/would/kindly at least once).
3. You include a short reply and a one-line follow-up.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable and professional.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Full email + clear request.
- [ ] Reply + follow-up.
- [ ] Polite tone.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-01/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Too direct ("Send it now").
- No subject or no closing.
- Slang at work.
