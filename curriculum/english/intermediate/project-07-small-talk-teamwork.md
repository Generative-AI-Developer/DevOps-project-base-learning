# Small Talk and Teamwork

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to make friendly small talk and support your team in English.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Before a meeting, you chat with a colleague. Warm small talk and a kind offer of help build a good relationship.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Small-talk questions (weekend, weather, hobbies).
- Past simple questions ("What did you do?").
- Teamwork phrases (support, praise).

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 6 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/07-small-talk-teamwork.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write **4 small-talk questions** you could ask a colleague.
2. Write a short **conversation** (6–8 lines) with small talk that leads into a teamwork moment (offer support or praise).

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] 4 small-talk questions.
- [ ] A 6–8 line friendly conversation.
- [ ] One past-simple question ("What did you do…?") used correctly.
- [ ] One teamwork line (support or praise).
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can start a warm conversation and support your teammates in English.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. You wrote 4 friendly, appropriate small-talk questions.
2. Your conversation uses at least one correct past-simple question.
3. It includes a teamwork line (offer support or praise).
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The tone is friendly and the English is clear.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] 4 small-talk questions.
- [ ] Past-simple question correct.
- [ ] Teamwork line.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-07/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "What did you did?" → "What did you do?"
- Questions too personal.
- Long, heavy answers as small talk.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Questions: "How was your weekend? Any plans for the weekend? Nice weather, isn't it?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Past simple: "What did you do at the weekend?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Teamwork: "Great job on the report! Let me know if you need support."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write one line that gently **encourages** a worried colleague: "Don't worry, we make a good team — we'll finish it together."

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your questions + conversation in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-07/`. Then say: **"I submit English Intermediate Project 7."**

**Remember:** Light topics + short friendly answers + support and praise = strong work relationships.
