# Describing Problems and Asking for Help

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to describe a work problem clearly and ask for help.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your computer keeps freezing and you can't access email. You must report it to IT support so they can fix it fast.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Describing what is wrong (what/when/need).
- Present continuous for problems ("isn't working").
- Asking for help politely.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 3 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/04-describing-problems.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **support conversation** (7–9 lines): describe a problem, answer questions, and ask for help.
2. Also write a short **support email/ticket** (3–5 sentences): what is wrong, when it started, and what you need.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A 7–9 line support conversation.
- [ ] A short support email/ticket.
- [ ] Use present continuous ("isn't working" / "keeps + -ing").
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can clearly report a problem and ask for help, in speech and in writing.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation and email describe **what** is wrong, **when** it started, and **what you need**.
2. You use present continuous correctly ("isn't working" or "keeps + -ing").
3. You ask for help politely.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Conversation + email.
- [ ] what/when/need included.
- [ ] Present continuous used.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-04/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "is not work" → "isn't working".
- Vague ("it's broken") — say what happens.
- No "when it started".

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Open: "I'm having a problem with my computer. It keeps freezing."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Detail: "There's an error message that says '…'. It started this morning."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Ask: "Could you help me fix it, please?"</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Add one line where **support** gives you a solution and you thank them: "Great, that fixed it. Thank you so much."

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your conversation + support email in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-04/`. Then say: **"I submit English Intermediate Project 4."**
