# Giving Updates and Status Reports

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to give a clear, short status update on your work.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your manager asks for a weekly update. You must clearly say what is done, in progress, pending, and the next steps.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Status words (done / in progress / pending).
- Present perfect for finished work.
- Naming blockers and next steps.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 4 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/05-status-updates.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Choose a real or example task/project.
2. Write a **status update** (email or short report) with: Done, In progress, Pending, a Blocker (if any), and Next steps.
3. Add an **estimate** of when it will finish.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Clear status: Done / In progress / Pending.
- [ ] A blocker and next steps.
- [ ] An estimate of the finish time.
- [ ] Use present perfect for a finished item ("I have completed…").
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
A clear, professional status update your manager could read in 20 seconds.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your update clearly shows Done, In progress, and Pending items.
2. It includes next steps and (if any) a blocker.
3. It uses present perfect for at least one finished item.
4. It gives an estimate for finishing.
5. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson, and the English is clear.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Done/In progress/Pending.
- [ ] Blocker + next steps.
- [ ] Present perfect used.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-05/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Unclear status.
- No next steps.
- "I finish" for a done task → "I have finished".

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Use the sample update in the study doc, section 4.</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write the update as if you are **behind schedule**: explain the delay politely and give a new estimate.

Save your status update in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-05/`. Then say: **"I submit English Intermediate Project 5."**
