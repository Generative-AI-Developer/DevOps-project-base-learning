# Phone Calls — Messages and Scheduling

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Learn to schedule, reschedule, and confirm details clearly on a work call.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You call to set a meeting, but the time doesn't work, so you reschedule and confirm the new time.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Offering times politely ("Would… be convenient?").
- Rescheduling.
- Repeating and confirming details.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Intermediate Lesson 2 completed.
- Read: `study/english/intermediate/03-phone-scheduling.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a **phone conversation** (8–10 lines) where you arrange a meeting, then **reschedule** it to a new time.
2. Include a polite time offer ("Would 3 p.m. be convenient?").
3. End by **repeating and confirming** the final time.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] An 8–10 line scheduling call.
- [ ] A reschedule to a new time.
- [ ] A polite time offer.
- [ ] A repeat-and-confirm line.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can set and change appointments on the phone and confirm details clearly.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your call arranges a meeting and then reschedules it.
2. It includes a polite offer ("Would… be convenient?").
3. It ends by repeating and confirming the final time.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable and polite.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Schedule + reschedule.
- [ ] Polite offer.
- [ ] Repeat-and-confirm.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/intermediate/project-03/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Not confirming the final time.
- "You are free Tuesday?" → "Are you available on Tuesday?"
- "Change the meeting" → "reschedule the meeting".

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Offer: "Would 3 p.m. be convenient?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Reschedule: "Can we reschedule to Thursday at 11 a.m.?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Confirm: "Let me repeat: Thursday, 11 a.m. Is that right?"</details>

## 12. Final challenge
