# English — Intermediate Level

Now you learn the English you need to **communicate at work**: emails, meetings, phone calls, and reports.

> **In simple words:** you move from everyday English to clear, polite, professional English for the office.

The main focus is still **vocabulary** and **practical use**, with a little grammar. Every lesson has a vocabulary table with **English and Urdu (اردو)** meanings, useful phrases, and a real work situation.

## The 8 lessons
1. Professional emails (requests, replies, attachments)
2. Meetings — joining and speaking up
3. Phone calls — messages and scheduling
4. Describing problems and asking for help
5. Giving updates and status reports
6. Making and handling requests politely
7. Small talk and teamwork
8. Run a short meeting / handle a work situation (level boss)

## Exit criteria
Finish all 8 lessons to complete English Intermediate. Then English Advanced unlocks.

**Remember:** At work, be **clear, short, and polite**. Practice each lesson by writing a real message you might send. 💼
