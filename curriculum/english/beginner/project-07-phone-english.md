# Basic Phone English

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 6)

## 1. Objective
Learn to answer a phone call politely and take a message.

## 2. Real-world scenario
At the office, the phone rings. The caller wants your manager, who is out. You must be polite, take a message, and promise to pass it on.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Phone greetings ("This is…").
- Asking who is calling.
- Taking a message and offering a callback.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lesson 6 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/07-phone-english.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and vocabulary.
2. Write a **phone conversation** (7–9 lines): you answer, the caller asks for someone who is out, and you take a message.
3. Write the **message note** you would leave (who called, for whom, and what they said).

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A 7–9 line phone conversation.
- [ ] Use "This is…" and "Who's calling, please?"
- [ ] Offer to take a message.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.
- [ ] Write a short message note.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can handle a basic phone call and take a clear message.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation uses "This is…" (not "I am…") and asks who is calling.
2. You offer to take a message when the person is not available.
3. You write a message note with: who called, for whom, and the message.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] "This is…" + "Who's calling?"
- [ ] Message taken.
- [ ] Message note written.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-07/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "I am Ali" on the phone → "This is Ali."
- "Who are you?" → "Who's calling, please?"
- "Wait" → "Hold on, please."

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Open: "Hello, this is [company]. How can I help you?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>If out: "He's not available right now. Can I take a message?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Message note: "Mr. Khan — Ali from XYZ called at 2 p.m. Please call him back. Urgent."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Add a polite line for a wrong number: "I'm sorry, there's no one by that name here. You may have the wrong number."

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your phone conversation + message note in `submissions/english/beginner/project-07/`. Then say: **"I submit English Beginner Project 7."**
