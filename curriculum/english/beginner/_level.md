# English — Beginner Level

Welcome to English! 🗣️ This level teaches the everyday English you need for daily life and your first days at work.

> **In simple words:** you will learn useful words, common phrases, and short real-life talks you can use right away.

The **main focus is vocabulary** (useful words) and **speaking/writing simple English**. Grammar is only a little, and always easy.

Every lesson gives you:
- **Words to know** — a vocabulary list with the **English meaning** and the **Urdu meaning (اردو)**.
- **Useful phrases** — ready sentences you can say.
- **A short conversation** — real life, like at an office.
- **A small grammar tip** — one easy point.
- **Practice** — you speak and write.

## The 8 lessons
1. Greetings and introductions
2. Everyday polite phrases
3. Numbers, time and dates
4. Talking about yourself and your job
5. Asking and giving directions
6. Shopping and money
7. Basic phone English
8. Write a simple email (level boss)

## How it works
Do the lessons in order. Each lesson has a small **task** (say it and write it). You **submit** your writing, I check it kindly, and the next lesson unlocks. 🔓

## Exit criteria
Finish all 8 lessons to complete English Beginner. Then English Intermediate unlocks.

**Remember:** Learn a few words every day. Say them out loud. Small daily practice makes you speak with confidence. 💪
