# Talking About Yourself and Your Job

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 3)

## 1. Objective
Learn to describe your job, your workplace, and your daily routine in simple English.

## 2. Real-world scenario
At a meeting or interview, someone asks: "Tell me about your job." You must answer clearly and confidently.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Words about jobs and work.
- Describing your role and daily routine.
- Present simple (add -s for he/she/it).

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lesson 3 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/04-yourself-and-job.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and vocabulary.
2. Write a short paragraph (5–7 sentences) about **your job**: your role, your company/department, your main tasks, and your daily routine.
3. Write a short **conversation** (5–6 lines) where someone asks about your job and you answer.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A 5–7 sentence paragraph about your job.
- [ ] Use present simple correctly (with -s for he/she/it at least once).
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.
- [ ] A conversation about your job.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can talk about your job and routine clearly and correctly.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your paragraph describes your role, workplace, and at least one daily task (5–7 sentences).
2. You use present simple correctly, including one he/she/it verb with **-s**.
3. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
4. Your conversation has a question about your job and your answer.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Paragraph about your job.
- [ ] -s used for he/she/it.
- [ ] 6+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-04/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "I am work…" → "I work…".
- "She work…" → "She works…".
- "years experience" → "years of experience".

<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Routine: "I start at 9 a.m. I check email, then I ___."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Add one sentence about a skill you want to learn next: "I want to learn ___."
