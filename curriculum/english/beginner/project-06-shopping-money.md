# Shopping and Money

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 5)

## 1. Objective
Learn to ask prices, pay, and talk about money while shopping.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You go to a shop (or buy office supplies). You must ask the price, ask for a discount, pay, and get a receipt.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Money and shopping words.
- Asking prices and paying.
- "How much" vs "How many".

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lesson 5 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/06-shopping-money.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and vocabulary.
2. Write a **shopping conversation** (6–8 lines): ask a price, ask for a discount, choose a payment (cash/card), and get a receipt.
3. Write 2 sentences using "How much…?" correctly.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A 6–8 line shopping conversation.
- [ ] Ask a price AND ask for a discount.
- [ ] Choose cash or card, and ask for a receipt.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can shop, ask prices, and pay in clear English.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your conversation asks a price and asks for a discount.
2. It includes a payment choice (cash/card) and a request for a receipt.
3. You use "How much…?" correctly (at least once).
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Price + discount asked.
- [ ] Payment + receipt.
- [ ] "How much" used correctly.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-06/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "How much cost?" → "How much does it cost?"
- "How many money" → "How much money".
- "I want buy" → "I want to buy".

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Start: "Excuse me, how much is this?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Discount: "Is there a discount?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Pay: "Can I pay by card? Can I have a receipt, please?"</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Add a line asking about a **refund**: "If it doesn't fit, can I get a refund?"

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your shopping conversation + sentences in `submissions/english/beginner/project-06/`. Then say: **"I submit English Beginner Project 6."**

**Remember:** "How much is it?" → pay (cash/card) → keep the receipt. How much = price; how many = count.
