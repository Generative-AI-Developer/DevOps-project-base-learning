# Asking and Giving Directions

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 4)

## 1. Objective
Learn to ask where a place is and to give simple directions.

## 2. Real-world scenario
A visitor asks you, "Where is the meeting room?" You must guide them clearly. And on your first day, you must ask where things are.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Asking "Where is the…?" / "How do I get to…?"
- Giving directions with commands (Go, Turn, Take).
- Place words: next to, opposite, between, near.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lesson 4 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/05-directions.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and vocabulary.
2. Write **directions** (3–5 steps) from your office entrance to your desk (or any room).
3. Write a short **conversation** (5–6 lines) where a visitor asks for a place and you give directions.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Directions with 3–5 steps.
- [ ] Use command verbs (Go / Turn / Take).
- [ ] Use at least **5 words** from today's list (include one place word like "next to").
- [ ] A conversation: someone asks, you guide them.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can ask for and give clear directions in an office or building.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your directions have 3–5 clear steps using command verbs (Go/Turn/Take).
2. You use at least one place word (next to / opposite / between / near) correctly.
3. You use at least **5 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
4. Your conversation has a question ("Where is the…?") and a helpful answer.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] 3–5 step directions.
- [ ] A place word used.
- [ ] 5+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-05/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "Where is exit?" → "Where is the exit?"
- "Turn on left" → "Turn left".
- Forgetting "the" before a place.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Ask: "Excuse me, where is the ___?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Answer: "Go straight, then turn right. It's next to the ___."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Floors: "Take the lift to the second floor."</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Give directions to TWO places in one answer (for example, the toilet and the exit).
