# Greetings and Introductions

**Status:** ▶️ Start
*(The real status lives in `progress.json`.)*

## 1. Objective
Learn to greet people and introduce yourself in simple, confident English.

## 2. Real-world scenario
It is your first day at a new office. You meet your manager and a colleague. You must say hello, tell your name and job, and be polite. Good first words make a good first impression.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Greetings for morning, afternoon, and evening.
- Introducing yourself (name + job).
- Polite words (please, thank you, sorry).
- The "to be" verb (am / is / are).

## 4. Prerequisites
- None. This is your first English lesson.
- Read first: `study/english/beginner/01-greetings-and-introductions.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and the vocabulary table.
2. Say the greeting out loud for morning, afternoon, and evening.
3. Write a short **self-introduction** (4–5 sentences): your name, your job, your office/team, and a polite ending.
4. Write a short **conversation** (4–6 lines) where you meet a new colleague.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Say greetings out loud (morning/afternoon/evening).
- [ ] Write a 4–5 sentence self-introduction.
- [ ] Use at least **5 words** from today's vocabulary list.
- [ ] Write a 4–6 line "meeting a colleague" conversation.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can greet and introduce yourself clearly, using the new words correctly and politely.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Your work passes if ALL of these are true:
1. Your self-introduction has your **name** and your **job** and is 4–5 sentences.
2. You use at least **5 words** from the lesson's vocabulary list (correctly).
3. You use "am / is / are" correctly (for example, "I **am** Ali", "She **is** my manager").
4. You include **one polite phrase** (please / thank you / nice to meet you).
5. Your conversation shows two people greeting and introducing themselves.
6. The English is understandable (small mistakes are OK — I will help you fix them).

## 9. Verification checklist (before you submit)
- [ ] My introduction has my name + job.
- [ ] I used 5+ new words.
- [ ] I used am/is/are correctly.
- [ ] I saved my writing in `submissions/english/beginner/project-01/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Saying "Good night" as a greeting (use "Good evening").
- "I am agree" → "I agree".
- Forgetting a polite word.
