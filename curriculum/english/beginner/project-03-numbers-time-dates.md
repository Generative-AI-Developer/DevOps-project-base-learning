# Numbers, Time and Dates

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 2)

## 1. Objective
Learn to say numbers, times, and dates, and to talk about when things happen.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your manager asks, "When can we meet?" You must give a time and a day clearly, like "at 11 a.m. on Tuesday."

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Numbers and time words.
- Telling the time (o'clock, a.m./p.m.).
- Dates and days.
- The small words at / on / in.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lesson 2 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/03-numbers-time-dates.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Read the lesson and vocabulary.
2. Write your **daily schedule** in English: at least 4 times with what you do (for example, "At 9 a.m., I start work.").
3. Write a short **conversation** (5–6 lines) where you and a colleague agree on a meeting time and day.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A daily schedule with 4+ times.
- [ ] Correct use of **at / on / in**.
- [ ] Use at least **5 words** from today's list.
- [ ] A conversation that fixes a time AND a day.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can say times and dates clearly and agree on a meeting time.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your schedule has at least 4 times, each with an activity.
2. You use **at** for times and **on** for days correctly (at least once each).
3. You use at least **5 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
4. Your conversation clearly sets a time and a day.
5. The English is understandable.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Schedule with 4+ times.
- [ ] at/on used correctly.
- [ ] 5+ new words.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-03/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- "in 3 o'clock" → "at 3 o'clock".
- "in Monday" → "on Monday".
- Mixing a.m. and p.m.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Schedule line: "At 9 a.m., I check my email."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Meeting: "Can we meet at 11 a.m. on Tuesday?"</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Remember: at = time, on = day, in = month/year.</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write one sentence with a date: "The report is due on the 15th of June."
