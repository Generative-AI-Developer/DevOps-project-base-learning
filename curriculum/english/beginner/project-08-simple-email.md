# Write a Simple Email (Level Boss 🏆)

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks after Lesson 7)

This is the **last lesson** of English Beginner. Finish it to complete the level and unlock English Intermediate.

## 1. Objective
Write a short, clear, polite email with all the correct parts.

## 2. Real-world scenario
You need to ask your manager for one day of leave (or send a short report). You must write a polite, professional email.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Email parts: subject, greeting, body, closing.
- Polite request language.
- Clear, short professional writing.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Lessons 1–7 completed.
- Read: `study/english/beginner/08-simple-email.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Choose one purpose: **ask for a day of leave** OR **send a short report/update**.
2. Write a complete email with: a **subject line**, a **greeting** ("Dear…"), a **body** (3–5 sentences), and a **closing** ("Best regards," + your name).
3. Make it polite and clear.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] A clear subject line.
- [ ] A greeting ("Dear [name],").
- [ ] A body of 3–5 sentences that explains your purpose.
- [ ] A polite closing with your name.
- [ ] Use at least **6 words** from today's list.

## 7. Expected outcome
A ready-to-send, polite email that a real manager would understand and accept.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes if ALL are true:
1. Your email has a **subject**, a **greeting**, a **body** (3–5 sentences), and a **closing** with your name.
2. It clearly states your purpose (leave request or report).
3. It is **polite** (uses "kindly"/"please"/"thank you") and starts sentences with capital letters.
4. You use at least **6 vocabulary words** from the lesson.
5. The English is understandable (I'll help fix small mistakes).

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Subject + greeting + body + closing.
- [ ] Purpose is clear.
- [ ] Polite + capital letters.
- [ ] Saved in `submissions/english/beginner/project-08/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- No subject line.
- Too casual ("Hey").
- No closing / no name.
- Long, unclear sentences.

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Use the sample email in `study/english/beginner/08-simple-email.md`, section 4, and change it to your purpose.</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Body start: "I hope you are well. I am writing to ___."</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Close with: "Thank you for your time. Best regards, [your name]".</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write a one-line **reply** you might get back (for example: "Dear Ali, That's fine. Enjoy your day off. Best regards, Mr. Khan").

Save your full email in `submissions/english/beginner/project-08/` (for example `email.md`). Then say: **"I submit English Beginner Project 8."** When it passes, English Intermediate unlocks! 🎉
