# CCAO-F Drill: GenAI and Claude Fundamentals

**Status:** 🔒 Locked (unlocks when the Anthropic Certification Level opens)

## 1. Objective
Show you understand the basics: what an LLM is, tokens, context, models, and hallucinations.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Before you can advise on Claude, you must know the words and ideas. Your team asks you to explain the basics clearly.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- LLM, token, context window.
- Model families and choosing.
- What AI is good/bad at.

## 4. Prerequisites
- Anthropic Advanced completed.
- Read: `study/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/00-ccao-f-guide.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write simple, correct one-line definitions for: LLM, token, context window, hallucination.
2. Explain when to pick Opus vs Sonnet vs Haiku (one line each).
3. List 3 things AI is good at and 3 to be careful about.
4. Answer 5 practice questions (from the study doc + your own).

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] 4 definitions correct.
- [ ] Model-choice explained.
- [ ] Good-at / careful-about lists.
- [ ] 5 practice questions answered.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can explain the fundamentals clearly and correctly.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes only if ALL are true:
1. Your definitions of LLM, token, context window, and hallucination are correct and simple.
2. Your Opus/Sonnet/Haiku guidance is correct (smart vs balanced vs fast/cheap).
3. Your "good at / be careful about" lists are sensible.
4. Your 5 practice-question answers are correct.
5. Everything is in simple English.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Definitions correct.
