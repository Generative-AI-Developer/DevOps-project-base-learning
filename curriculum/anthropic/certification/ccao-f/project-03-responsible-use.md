# CCAO-F Drill: Responsible and Safe Use

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

## 1. Objective
Show you understand safe, responsible use of AI: privacy, bias, hallucinations, and human review.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your company must use AI safely. You help set the rules: what to share, what to check, when a human must review.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Protect private data.
- Spot and reduce hallucinations.
- Know about bias.
- Keep humans in the loop.

## 4. Prerequisites
- CCAO-F Project 2 completed.
- Read: `study/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/00-ccao-f-guide.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Write a short "safe use" guide (5 rules) for your team.
2. Give 2 examples of data you should NOT paste into an AI tool, and why.
3. Show one case where the AI could hallucinate, and how you would check it.
4. Explain when a human MUST review the output (2 examples).

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] 5-rule safe-use guide.
- [ ] 2 "do not paste" examples.
- [ ] A hallucination check.
- [ ] 2 human-review cases.

## 7. Expected outcome
You can set and explain responsible-use rules for AI at work.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes only if ALL are true:
1. Your safe-use guide has 5 clear, correct rules.
2. Your "do not paste" examples are sensible (secrets, personal data, etc.) with reasons.
3. You show a realistic hallucination risk AND a way to check it.
4. Your human-review cases are appropriate (high-stakes, legal, medical, etc.).
5. Simple English throughout.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Safe-use guide.
- [ ] Privacy examples.
- [ ] Hallucination check.
- [ ] Human-review cases.
- [ ] Evidence saved in `submissions/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/project-03/`.

## 10. Common mistakes
- Assuming AI is always right.
- Pasting secrets/personal data.
- No human review for high-stakes outputs.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Rules like: check important facts, don't share secrets, review high-stakes outputs, know AI can be biased.</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>Do-not-paste: passwords, customer personal data, confidential contracts.</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Human review: medical, legal, money, or safety decisions.</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Connect to your DevOps security mindset: write 3 ways "least privilege" and "don't trust outside input" (from CKS and the guardrails project) also apply to responsible AI use.

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your guide, examples, and cases in `submissions/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/project-03/`. Then say: **"I submit CCAO-F Project 3."**
