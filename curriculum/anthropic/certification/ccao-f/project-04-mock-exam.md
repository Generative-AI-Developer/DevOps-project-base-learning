# CCAO-F: Take the Mock Exam

**Status:** 🔒 Locked

This is the **last CCAO-F project**. Pass the mock (with no weak domain) to become **CCAO-F ready** and unlock CCDV-F.

## 1. Objective
Sit a timed knowledge mock covering every CCAO-F domain, and fix any weak areas.

## 2. Real-world scenario
Your rehearsal for the Associate exam: 60 minutes of knowledge questions.

## 3. Skills and concepts you will learn
- Exam pacing for a knowledge test.
- Weak-domain review.

## 4. Prerequisites
- CCAO-F Projects 1–3 completed.
- Read: `study/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/00-ccao-f-guide.md`.

## 5. Step-by-step requirements
1. Open `exams/anthropic/ccao-f/mock-1.md`. Set a 60-minute timer.
2. Answer all 15 questions.
3. Save your answers under `submissions/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/mock-1/`.
4. Ask the mentor to grade with `exams/anthropic/ccao-f/rubric-1.md`.

## 6. Tasks / challenges
- [ ] Mock attempted within 60 min.
- [ ] Answers saved.
- [ ] Domain scores recorded.
- [ ] Weak areas fixed.

## 7. Expected outcome
A score per domain and a clear picture of readiness.

## 8. Acceptance criteria (how I grade it)
Passes only if ALL are true:
1. You attempted the mock within the time limit and saved your answers.
2. The mentor grades it and writes `domainScores` into `progress.json`.
3. **Overall ≥ 72%** AND **no domain below 66%**.
4. Any weak domain was fixed (redo its drill, retake) before this passes.

## 9. Verification checklist
- [ ] Mock graded.
- [ ] No weak domain.
- [ ] Evidence saved in `submissions/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/project-04/` (+ mock folder).

## 10. Common mistakes
- Rushing and misreading questions.
- Skipping the review of wrong answers.

## 11. Hints
<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Read each question fully; watch for NOT/EXCEPT.</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2</summary>When unsure, pick the safe, responsible answer.</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3</summary>Weak domain? Redo Projects 1–3 for that area and retake.</details>

## 12. Final challenge
Write your own 5 practice questions (with answers) for a topic you found hard. Teaching yourself locks it in.

## 13. What to submit (evidence)
Save your answers in `submissions/anthropic/certification/ccao-f/`. Then say: **"I submit CCAO-F Project 4 (Mock Exam)."** When it passes with no weak domain, CCDV-F unlocks! 🎉
