# Kubernetes — Intermediate Level

Now you learn the skills that make up most of the **CKAD** exam. These are the daily tools of a Kubernetes developer.

> **In simple words:** you move from "run an app" to "run an app well" — healthy, configured, stored, and connected.

## What you will learn
1. Health probes: liveness, readiness, startup (Project 1).
2. Resource requests and limits (Project 2).
3. Init containers and sidecars (Project 3).
4. Jobs and CronJobs (Project 4).
5. Persistent storage: PV and PVC (Project 5).
6. StatefulSets (Project 6).
7. Ingress (Project 7).
8. A multi-service app, fully wired (Project 8 — level boss).

## Exit criteria
Finish all 8 projects to complete Kubernetes Intermediate. Then Kubernetes Advanced unlocks.

**Remember:** These 8 topics are CKAD gold. Practice each one until it is fast and easy.
