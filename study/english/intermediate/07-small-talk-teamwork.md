# Study: Small Talk and Teamwork

Good work relationships start with friendly words. This lesson helps you make **small talk** and be a good team member.

## 1. Easy explanation
**Small talk** is short, friendly chat (about the weekend, the weather, or work). It builds warm relationships. **Teamwork** words help you support your colleagues and work well together.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- How was your **weekend**?
- Any plans for the **weekend**?
- Nice **weather** today.
- How's the project going?
- **Great job** on that!
- Let me know if you need **support**.
- We make a good **team**.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Small talk before a meeting.**
- **You:** Good morning! How was your weekend?
- **Colleague:** It was nice, thanks. I visited my family. And you?
- **You:** Relaxing, thank you. Ready for the meeting?
- **Colleague:** Yes. I'm a little worried about the deadline.
- **You:** Don't worry — we make a good team. Let me know if you need support.
- **Colleague:** Thank you, I appreciate that.

## 5. Small grammar tip — past simple for the weekend
To ask about the past, use **did + base verb**:
- "What **did** you **do** at the weekend?" (not "What did you did")
- Answer in past simple: "I **visited** my family." · "I **watched** a movie."

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "How was your weekend? — It was nice, thanks."
2. Write 4 small-talk questions you can ask a colleague.
3. Write 2 teamwork lines (offer support, praise good work).

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "What did you did?" → ✅ "What **did** you **do**?"
- ❌ Very personal questions (money, age). → ✅ Keep it light: weekend, weather, hobbies.
- ❌ A long life story as small talk. → ✅ Keep answers short and friendly.

## 8. Tips to remember
- Safe topics: **weekend, weather, hobbies, work**.
- Praise helps teams: **"Great job!"**, **"Well done!"**
- Offer support: **"Let me know if you need help."**

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Fix: "What did you watched?"
2. Give a friendly small-talk question.
3. Praise a colleague's work in one line.
