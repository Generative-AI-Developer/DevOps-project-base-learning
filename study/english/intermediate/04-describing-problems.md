# Study: Describing Problems and Asking for Help

At work, things break — a computer, a printer, a login. This lesson helps you describe the problem clearly and ask for help.

## 1. Easy explanation
To get help fast, describe the problem **clearly**: what is broken, what happens, and what you need. Clear words help the other person fix it quickly.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- I'm having a **problem** with my computer.
- My laptop **isn't working**.
- There's an **error** message on the screen.
- It **keeps crashing / freezing**.
- I **can't access** my email.
- Could you help me **fix** it?
- Have you tried **restarting** it?

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Reporting an IT problem.**
- **You:** Hi, I'm having a problem with my computer. It keeps freezing.
- **Support:** Okay. When did it start?
- **You:** This morning. There's also an error message that says "no connection".
- **Support:** Have you tried restarting it?
- **You:** Yes, twice. It's still slow and I can't access my email.
- **Support:** Understood. I'll check the network. I think I have a solution.

## 5. Small grammar tip — problems happening now
Use **present continuous** (am/is/are + verb-ing) for a problem right now:
- "My computer **is not working**." ("isn't working")
- "It **is crashing** again." · "The internet **is being** slow."
- Use **keeps + verb-ing** for something that happens again and again: "It **keeps freezing**."

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "My computer isn't working. It keeps freezing."
2. Describe a real problem you had (3–4 sentences): what, when, and what you need.
3. Write one line asking for help politely.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "My computer is not work." → ✅ "My computer **isn't working**."
- ❌ "It crash always." → ✅ "It **keeps crashing**."
- ❌ Vague: "It's broken." → ✅ Say **what** happens: "It freezes when I open email."

## 8. Tips to remember
- Describe: **what** + **when** + **what you need**.
- Use **"isn't working"** and **"keeps + -ing"** for problems.
- Give the exact **error message** if there is one.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Fix: "The printer is not work."
2. Say a problem that happens again and again.
3. Ask politely for help to fix something.
