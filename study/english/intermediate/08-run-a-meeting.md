# Study: Running a Short Meeting

Sometimes you must **lead** a short meeting: start it, keep it on track, assign tasks, and wrap up. This lesson gives you the language to do it.

## 1. Easy explanation
To run a meeting: **open** it, follow the **agenda**, let people speak, **assign** tasks, and **wrap up** with a clear **recap** (a short summary of who does what by when).

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Let's **get started**.
- The **agenda** today is: 1… 2… 3…
- What are your thoughts?
- Let's **assign** the tasks. Who can take this?
- I'll **delegate** this to **[name]**.
- Let's **wrap up**.
- To **recap**, the **action items** are… by **[deadline]**.

## 4. A sample short meeting
```
You (chair): Good morning, everyone. Let's get started.
             Today's agenda: the deadline, and task sharing.

You: First, the deadline. We're a little behind. Any ideas?
Sara: We could split the testing to save time.
You: Good idea. Let's assign tasks. Ali, can you take testing?
Ali: Sure, I'll do it.
You: Great. Sara, please coordinate with the client.
Sara: Will do.
You: To recap: Ali does testing, Sara handles the client,
     both by Thursday. Thank you all. Let's wrap up.
```

## 5. Small grammar tip — "Let's + verb" for group suggestions
Use **Let's** (Let us) + base verb to suggest doing something together:
- "**Let's** get started." · "**Let's** assign the tasks." · "**Let's** wrap up."

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "Let's get started. The agenda today is…"
2. Write an opening line and a recap line for a meeting.
3. Assign one task to a person with a deadline.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ No recap at the end. → ✅ Always recap: who does what, by when.
- ❌ "Let's to start." → ✅ "**Let's start**." (no "to")
- ❌ No deadlines on tasks. → ✅ Add a deadline: "…by Thursday."

## 8. Tips to remember
- Structure: **Open → Agenda → Discuss → Assign → Recap → Wrap up**.
- Every task needs a **person** and a **deadline**.
- End with a clear **recap** so everyone knows what to do.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Open a meeting in one line.
2. Assign a task with a deadline.
3. Give a recap of two action items.
