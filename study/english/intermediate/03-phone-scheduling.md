# Study: Phone Calls — Messages and Scheduling

At work, many calls are about time: setting, changing, and confirming appointments. This lesson helps you schedule and take clear details on the phone.

## 1. Easy explanation
On a work call you often **schedule** (set a time), **reschedule** (change it), or **confirm** details. You also **spell** names and **repeat** information to be sure it is correct.

## 2. Words to know (vocabulary)

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Are you **available** on Tuesday?
- Would 3 p.m. **be convenient**?
- Let me check my **schedule**.
- Can we **reschedule** to Thursday?
- Could you **spell** your name, please?
- Let me **repeat** that to **confirm**.
- I'll **transfer** you to the right person.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Scheduling and confirming a call.**
- **You:** Hello, this is Ali. I'd like to arrange a call with Mr. Khan.
- **Assistant:** Sure. Are you available on Tuesday?
- **You:** Tuesday works. Would 3 p.m. be convenient?
- **Assistant:** Let me check the schedule… Yes, 3 p.m. is free.
- **You:** Great. Could you confirm by email too?
- **Assistant:** Of course. Let me repeat: Tuesday, 3 p.m., with Mr. Khan.
- **You:** Perfect. Thank you.

## 5. Small grammar tip — polite offers with "Would… / Let me…"
- **Would** 3 p.m. be convenient? (polite question)
- **Let me** check the schedule. (offering to do something)
- **Could** you spell that, please? (polite request)

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "Would 3 p.m. be convenient? Let me check my schedule."
2. Write a call to **reschedule** a meeting to a new day/time.
3. Write one line to confirm details by repeating them.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "You are free Tuesday?" → ✅ "**Are** you **available** on Tuesday?"
- ❌ Not confirming → mistakes. → ✅ "Let me repeat that to confirm."
- ❌ "Change the meeting." → ✅ "Can we **reschedule** the meeting?"

## 8. Tips to remember
- Always **repeat and confirm** times and names.
- Use **"Would… be convenient?"** to offer a time politely.
- Offer to **confirm by email** — it avoids mistakes.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Ask politely if 4 p.m. is suitable.
2. What word means "change to a new time"?
3. Write a line to confirm a name by spelling.
