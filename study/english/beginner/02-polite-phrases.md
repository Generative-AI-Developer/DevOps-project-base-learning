# Study: Everyday Polite Phrases

Polite words make people like you and want to help you. This lesson gives you the small, kind words you use every day at work and in life.

## 1. Easy explanation
Being **polite** means using kind, respectful words. When you say "please", "thank you", and "sorry" at the right time, people trust you and enjoy working with you.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Could you help me, **please**?
- Thank you so much. — You're welcome.
- Excuse me, may I ask a question?
- Sorry to bother you.
- No problem at all.
- I really **appreciate** your help.
- Pardon? Could you say that again, please?

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Asking a colleague for help.**
- **You:** Excuse me, Sara. Sorry to bother you. Could you help me, please?
- **Sara:** Of course! What do you need?
- **You:** How do I send this file to the manager?
- **Sara:** Click here, then press send. Like this.
- **You:** Thank you so much. I really appreciate it.
- **Sara:** You're welcome. No problem.

## 5. Small grammar tip — polite requests
Make requests soft with **Could you…?** and **May I…?**
- Direct: *Give me the pen.* (sounds rude)
- Polite: **Could you** give me the pen, **please**?
- Polite: **May I** use your phone?

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say out loud: "Could you help me, please?" and "Thank you so much." (3 times)
2. Write 3 polite requests using "Could you…" or "May I…".
3. Reply to "Thank you" in two ways.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "Give me water." → ✅ "Could you give me some water, please?"
- ❌ Saying "sorry" for everything. → ✅ Use "excuse me" to get attention; save "sorry" for real mistakes.
- ❌ "Thank you" with no reply. → ✅ Reply "You're welcome." or "No problem."

## 8. Tips to remember
- Add **"please"** to any request — it instantly sounds polite.
- Learn the pair: **"Thank you" → "You're welcome."**
- Smile when you speak; it makes your words kinder.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Turn "Open the door." into a polite request.
2. What do you say to reply to "Thank you"?
3. Which word means "please say it again"?
