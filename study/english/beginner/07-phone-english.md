# Study: Basic Phone English

On the phone you cannot see the person, so clear words matter a lot. This lesson gives you the phrases to answer calls and take messages.

## 1. Easy explanation
On the phone, English is a little special. You say "**This is** Ali" (not "I am Ali"). You ask "**May I speak to** Sara?" and "**Who's calling?**" You also learn to **take a message** when someone is not there.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Hello, **this is** Ali.
- **May I speak to** Sara, please?
- **Who's calling**, please?
- **Hold on**, please. I'll connect you.
- He's not **available** right now.
- Can I **take a message**?
- Can you **call back later**?
- I'll call you back.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Answering a call and taking a message.**
- **You:** Hello, this is ABC Company. How can I help you?
- **Caller:** Hello, may I speak to Mr. Khan, please?
- **You:** Who's calling, please?
- **Caller:** This is Ali from XYZ.
- **You:** Hold on, please… Sorry, he's not available right now. Can I take a message?
- **Caller:** Yes. Please ask him to call me back. It's a little urgent.
- **You:** Sure. I'll give him the message. Thank you for calling.

## 5. Small grammar tip — phone language
- On the phone, say **"This is [name]"**, not "I am [name]".
- Ask politely: **"May I speak to…?"** and **"Who's calling, please?"**

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "Hello, this is ___. May I speak to ___, please?"
2. Write a phone talk (6–8 lines) where you take a message.
3. Write one polite line to ask someone to call back.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "I am Ali." (on phone) → ✅ "**This is** Ali."
- ❌ "Who are you?" → ✅ "**Who's calling**, please?"
- ❌ "Wait." → ✅ "**Hold on**, please."

## 8. Tips to remember
- Phone start: **"Hello, this is ___."**
- If the person is out, offer: **"Can I take a message?"**
- Speak slowly and clearly — the caller can't see you.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. How do you say your name on the phone?
2. Ask politely who is calling.
3. Offer to take a message.
