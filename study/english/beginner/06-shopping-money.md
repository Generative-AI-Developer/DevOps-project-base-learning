# Study: Shopping and Money

Every day you buy things and talk about money. This lesson gives you the words to shop and pay with confidence.

## 1. Easy explanation
When you shop, you ask the **price** ("How much is this?"), you **pay** (cash or card), and you take your **change** and **receipt**. These words are useful in shops, at work, and online.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- How much is this?
- How much does it cost?
- Can I pay by card / in cash?
- Here is your change.
- Can I have a receipt, please?
- Is there a discount?
- That's too expensive. Do you have a cheaper one?

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Buying something at a shop.**
- **You:** Excuse me, how much is this bag?
- **Seller:** It's 1,500 rupees.
- **You:** That's a bit expensive. Is there a discount?
- **Seller:** I can give you 10% off. So 1,350.
- **You:** Okay, I'll take it. Can I pay by card?
- **Seller:** Yes. Here is your receipt. Thank you.

## 5. Small grammar tip — "How much" vs "How many"
- **How much** for money and things you can't count: *How much money? How much is it?*
- **How many** for things you can count: *How many bags? How many people?*

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "How much is this? Can I pay by card?"
2. Write a short shopping talk (5–6 lines) with a price and a payment.
3. Ask for a discount and a receipt.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "How much cost?" → ✅ "How much does it **cost**?"
- ❌ "How many money?" → ✅ "How **much** money?"
- ❌ "I want buy this." → ✅ "I want **to** buy this."

## 8. Tips to remember
- **How much** = price/uncountable; **How many** = countable.
- Always ask for a **receipt** — it helps with a **refund** later.
- Learn the pair: **cheap ↔ expensive**.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Fix: "How much this costs?"
2. Ask to pay with a card.
3. What word means "money taken off the price"?
