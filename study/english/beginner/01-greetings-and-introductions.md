# Study: Greetings and Introductions

When you meet people — at work, in a shop, or anywhere — you start with a **greeting** and an **introduction**. This lesson gives you the words and sentences to do it with confidence.

## 1. Easy explanation
A **greeting** is a friendly hello (like "Good morning"). An **introduction** is when you tell your name and hear theirs ("My name is Ali"). These are the first words of almost every talk, so they are very useful.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Hello, my name is **[your name]**.
- Nice to meet you.
- How are you? — I'm fine, thank you. And you?
- This is my colleague, **[name]**.
- I work as a **[your job]**.
- Where do you work?
- Goodbye. See you later.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**At the office — Ali meets a new colleague, Sara.**
- **Ali:** Good morning! My name is Ali. Nice to meet you.
- **Sara:** Good morning, Ali. I'm Sara. Nice to meet you too.
- **Ali:** Are you new here?
- **Sara:** Yes, today is my first day. I work as an accountant.
- **Ali:** Welcome to the team! If you need help, please ask me.
- **Sara:** Thank you, Ali. That is very kind.

## 5. Small grammar tip — "am / is / are"
Use the correct "to be" word:
- **I am** (I'm) → *I am Ali.*
- **You are** (you're) → *You are new.*
- **He / She is** → *She is my manager.*
- **They are** → *They are my colleagues.*

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say out loud: "Hello, my name is ___. Nice to meet you." (3 times)
2. Write 3 sentences about yourself: your name, your job, your office.
3. Practice a greeting for morning, afternoon, and evening.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "I am agree." → ✅ "I agree." (Do not use *am* with *agree*.)
- ❌ Using "Good night" to say hello. → ✅ "Good night" is only for leaving or going to sleep. Use "Good evening" as a greeting.
- ❌ "How are you?" → answering with a long story. → ✅ Keep it short: "I'm fine, thank you."

## 8. Tips to remember
- Learn **5 words a day**, not 50. Small and daily wins.
- **Say words out loud** — your mouth must practice, not only your eyes.
- Use a new word **the same day** in a real sentence.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. What greeting do you use in the morning?
2. Write the polite word you say when you ask for something.
3. Make one sentence: introduce your colleague to your manager.
