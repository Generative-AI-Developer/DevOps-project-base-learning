# Study: Numbers, Time and Dates

Every day you talk about time: meetings, appointments, and dates. This lesson gives you the words to say **when**.

## 1. Easy explanation
You need numbers to say prices, times, and dates. You need time words to say when something happens ("at 3 o'clock", "tomorrow", "on Monday").

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- What time is it? — It's 3 o'clock.
- The meeting is **at** 10 a.m.
- I have an appointment **at** 2 p.m.
- What's the date today?
- See you **tomorrow**.
- Let's meet **on** Monday.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Setting a meeting time.**
- **You:** Are you free tomorrow?
- **Colleague:** Yes. What time?
- **You:** Can we meet at 11 o'clock in the morning?
- **Colleague:** 11 a.m. is good. Which day — is that Tuesday?
- **You:** Yes, Tuesday. Thank you. See you then.

## 5. Small grammar tip — at / on / in
- **at** + a time → *at 3 o'clock, at 9 a.m.*
- **on** + a day/date → *on Monday, on the 5th.*
- **in** + a month/year → *in June, in 2026.*

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "The meeting is at 10 a.m. on Monday."
2. Write your daily schedule for 3 times (for example, 9 a.m., 1 p.m., 5 p.m.).
3. Write today's date in English.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "The meeting is **in** 3 o'clock." → ✅ "**at** 3 o'clock."
- ❌ "See you **in** Monday." → ✅ "See you **on** Monday."
- ❌ Mixing a.m. (morning) and p.m. (afternoon/evening) — 2 a.m. is night; 2 p.m. is afternoon.

## 8. Tips to remember
- **at** = exact time, **on** = day, **in** = month/year.
- a.m. = before noon, p.m. = after noon.
- Practice saying the current time every hour today.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Fill in: "The meeting is ___ 9 a.m. ___ Friday."
2. What is the day after today called?
3. Say your office start time in English.
