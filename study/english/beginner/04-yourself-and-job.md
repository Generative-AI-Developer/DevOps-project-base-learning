# Study: Talking About Yourself and Your Job

People often ask, "What do you do?" This lesson helps you talk about your job, your work, and your daily routine.

## 1. Easy explanation
When you meet people at work, you tell them your **job** (what you do), your **company** (where you work), and your **daily routine** (what you do each day). This is very common and useful.

## 2. Words to know (vocabulary)

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- I work at **[company]**.
- I am a / an **[job]**.
- My job is to **[main task]**.
- I have **[number]** years of experience.
- I start work at 9 and finish at 5.
- I am responsible **for [task]**.
- I am learning **[skill]**.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**Telling someone about your job.**
- **New person:** What do you do?
- **You:** I am a software developer. I work at a tech company.
- **New person:** Nice! What is your role?
- **You:** I write code and fix problems. I am responsible for the website.
- **New person:** How long have you worked there?
- **You:** For two years. I really enjoy it, and I am always learning.

## 5. Small grammar tip — present simple (routines)
Use present simple for things you do regularly. For **he / she / it**, add **-s**:
- I **work**, you **work**, we **work**, they **work**.
- He **works**, she **manages**, it **starts**.

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say 3 times: "I am a ___. I work at ___. My job is to ___."
2. Write 5 sentences about your job and daily routine.
3. Write one sentence about a skill you are learning.

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ "I am work at a bank." → ✅ "I **work** at a bank."
- ❌ "She work here." → ✅ "She **works** here." (add -s for she/he/it)
- ❌ "I have 3 years experience." → ✅ "I have 3 years **of** experience."

## 8. Tips to remember
- For **he/she/it**, the verb gets **-s** (works, starts, manages).
- Learn one sentence about your job by heart and say it fast.
- Add "I am responsible for…" to sound professional.

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Fix: "She work in the office."
2. Complete: "I have five years ___ experience."
3. Say what time you start and finish work.
