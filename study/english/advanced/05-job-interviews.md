# Study: Job Interviews — Skills and Experience

An interview is your chance to show your **skills** and **experience**. This lesson helps you answer common questions with confidence.

## 1. Easy explanation
In an interview, you talk about your **experience** (what you've done), your **strengths** (what you're good at), and your **achievements** (what you've accomplished). Speak clearly, be confident but not boastful, and give real examples.

## 3. Useful phrases (ready to use)
- Tell me about yourself. → I'm a **[job]** with **[X]** years of **experience**.
- My **strength** is **[skill]**.
- One **achievement** I'm proud of is **[result]**.
- I work well in a **team** and I'm **reliable**.
- I'm **motivated** to **[goal]**.
- Why should we **hire** you? → Because I can **contribute** **[value]**.

## 4. A real-life conversation
**A short interview.**
- **Interviewer:** Tell me about yourself.
- **You:** I'm a software developer with three years of experience. I've built websites and fixed complex bugs.
- **Interviewer:** What is your greatest strength?
- **You:** My strength is problem-solving. For example, I once fixed a bug that saved my team two days of work.
- **Interviewer:** And a weakness?
- **You:** I used to take on too much myself. Now I've learned to delegate and ask for help.
- **Interviewer:** Why should we hire you?
- **You:** Because I'm reliable, motivated, and I can contribute strong technical skills to your team.

## 5. Small grammar tip — present perfect for experience
Use **have + past participle** for your experience (past that connects to now):
- "I **have worked** in two companies."
- "I **have built** several websites."
- "I **have learned** to manage my time."

## 6. Practice — say it and write it
1. Say your "Tell me about yourself" answer out loud (3 sentences).
2. Write an answer for "What is your strength?" with an example.
3. Write a smart "weakness" answer (a real weakness + how you improved it).

## 7. Common mistakes (and the fix)
- ❌ Too boastful ("I'm the best."). → ✅ Give a real **example** instead.
- ❌ "My weakness is I work too hard." (sounds fake). → ✅ Give a real weakness + how you're improving it.
- ❌ "I worked here since 2 years." → ✅ "I **have worked** here **for** 2 years."

## 8. Tips to remember
- For achievements, use an example: **situation → what you did → result**.
- Be confident but honest.
- Prepare answers for: yourself, strength, weakness, "why hire you".

## 9. Practice questions / tasks
1. Answer "Tell me about yourself" in 2 sentences.
2. State a strength with an example.
3. Fix: "I work here since 3 years."
